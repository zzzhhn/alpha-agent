# Alpha Agent

## Register

product

## Users

Desktop-first personal investors and factor researchers. Chinese and English;
dark and light themes. The investor workflow must remain usable without writing
factor expressions. The researcher workflow includes local tests and BRAIN.

## Product Purpose

Help users inspect evidence, record decisions and validate strategies on paper.
Reliability and a passing research check are not proof of investment profit.
No-action, missing evidence and historical exceptions are legitimate outcomes.

## Brand Personality

Evidence-led, restrained, explicit. Keep the existing AlphaCore workstation.
The approved reference is the production Design System, not a new palette.

## Anti-references

Do not replace workflows with marketing cards, tiny labels, unbounded lists,
decorative metrics, opaque English headings in Chinese mode or profit promises.

## Design Principles

- Match policy, date, scope and source before comparing numbers.
- Use one canonical control vocabulary and progressive disclosure.
- Keep private planning and global administration distinct.
- Explain the cause and recovery action of missing or failed results.
- Preserve research evidence and user ownership of consequential decisions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve readable theme tokens, keyboard controls, text explanations alongside
color, a minimum visible type floor, and locale-aware labels. Desktop is the
primary context; narrow screens must remain readable without page overflow.

## Canonical visual specification

Use `docs/design-system/ALPHA-DESIGN-SYSTEM-ASSET-CATALOG.md`, production `tm`
components and `frontend/src/app/globals.css`. They remain authoritative; this
context document adds no new styles. Design entry point: `integrate-design`.
